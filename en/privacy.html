Privacy

This website collects no personal data and sets no cookies.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no embedded social buttons, no fonts or scripts loaded from third-party services. That is why no cookie banner is shown.

Language preference

If you pick a language with the language button, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Technical logs

As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub, Inc., see the legal notice) may record technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) needed for security and for the service to work. LAIION Labs does not use them for any other purpose.

If you email us

The information in your email is used only to reply to you. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time at contact@laiionlabs.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Liber AUTO apps

Data processing in the Liber AUTO and Liber AUTO Pro apps is described in their own policy: liberauto.eu/legal/confidentialite.
