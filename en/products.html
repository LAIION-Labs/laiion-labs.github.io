Consumers

Liber AUTO

Vehicle classifieds across the European Union.

Vehicles
Cars, motorcycles, boats, agricultural and construction machinery, aircraft.
Sellers
Listings from verified dealers, marked with the “Verified by Liber AUTO” badge, and from private sellers.
Coverage
The whole European Union: 27 countries, 24 languages.
Features
Detailed search and alerts, messaging with sellers, viewing and test-drive requests, dealer reviews.
Availability
Available on the web now. Android and iOS apps: coming soon to the stores.

Professionals

Liber AUTO Pro

Management software for vehicle professionals.

Management
Vehicle stock, documents, finances and sales.
Artificial intelligence
Automatic reading of registration certificates, invoices and VIN numbers.
Electronic police register
Provided for by articles R321-3 et seq. of the French Criminal Code. Entries cannot be altered, access log, PDF and CSV exports.
Publishing
List vehicles on Liber AUTO in one step, with featured placements.
Team and follow-up
Test drives and viewings, messaging, team management, statistics, multiple locations.
Plans
Subscription with a 14-day free trial. Current pricing on liberauto.eu.
Platforms
Web, Android and iOS.
