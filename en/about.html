About

A lab that builds its own products

LAIION Labs is a technology lab focused on innovation and the integration of artificial intelligence.

The company

LAIION Labs is a French simplified joint-stock company with a sole shareholder (SASU), founded in 2026 and registered with the Paris Trade and Companies Register. Its head office is in Paris.

Our mission

To design useful, reliable and compliant digital products, and to keep them running and improving over time. Artificial intelligence is a tool for concrete tasks, not an end in itself.

Our own products, no custom work

LAIION Labs designs, builds, hosts and develops its own apps and websites. We do not take on custom development projects for clients.

Our first products are Liber AUTO, a vehicle marketplace for the whole European Union, and Liber AUTO Pro, management software for vehicle professionals. See the products.
